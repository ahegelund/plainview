# Tax Framework (Denmark 2026)

**Classification**: Hobby Business (Hobbyvirksomhed)
**Jurisdiction**: Copenhagen

## 1. Classification Rules
- **Non-Commercial**: Due to lack of profitability and scale, this is a hobby business.
- **Tax Liabilities**:
    - **AM-bidrag**: 8% on gross income (profit).
    - **Personal Income Tax**: ~35.4% (Bottom + Municipal).
    - **Deductions**: Operating costs (electricity, hardware depreciation) are deductible.
    - **Losses**: **Cannot be deducted** against other income (e.g., salary).

## 2. Effective Tax Rate
The layered tax rate for 2026 personal income:
1. Gross Profit
2. Less 8% AM-bidrag
3. Remaining 92% taxed at ~35.4%
4. **Effective Rate**: **~40.6%**

## 3. The "Profit" Trap
Since costs (electricity + depreciation) are deductible:
- If Gross Profit > 0: You pay 40.6% on the profit.
- If Gross Profit < 0: **You eat the loss.** No tax credit.

> [!NOTE]
> Given the "Costs & Gross Profit" analysis suggests a likely negative gross margin, the tax framework basically becomes irrelevant as there is no profit to tax, but the loss is fully realized by the user.
